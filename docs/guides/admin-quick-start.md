# Admin console quick start (staff operations)

This guide describes the staff console at `/admin` as it is built today. Every button you see is
also checked on the server: the navigation and buttons only hide what your role cannot do, and a
refused action shows the server's reason in place. Money is shown in naira and stored as integer
kobo; unknown values read "unknown" and are never estimated.

Demo accounts (development only, password `DemoPassword-2026!`, created by
`pnpm --filter @simplexd/web seed:demo`): `admin@`, `ops@`, `pm@`, `inspector@`, `finance@` and
`support@demo.simplexd.local`; customers `owner@`/`member@demo.simplexd.local`.

## Before you start

- **Authenticator (MFA).** Finance actions (issuing or voiding invoices, reconciliation, bank
  transfer confirmation, refund approval, payout approvals, exports), opening sealed bids and
  milestone payment authorisation require a verified authenticator. Enrol at
  _Security → authenticator_ (`/admin/security/mfa`). Without it the pages still load and say what
  is blocked, with a link to enrol.
- **Saved views.** Most tables have a filter bar whose state lives in the URL. _Save current
  filters_ names the view and keeps it in this browser; share a view by copying the link.
- **Exports.** _Export CSV_ downloads exactly the rows on screen. Finance allocation exports are
  generated by the server and checked against receipts first (see Finance).
- **Bulk actions.** Where offered (service request triage/assignment, issuing draft invoices),
  select rows, choose the action and read the preview: rows that would be skipped are listed with
  the reason, and nothing runs until you confirm. Each row is a separate server call and its
  outcome is shown.
- Light, dark and system themes follow the toggle in the header; every page works at 360 px and
  with the keyboard (dialogs trap focus; Escape closes them).

## Service requests (`/admin/service-requests`)

The SLA queue lists every customer organisation's requests; overdue and due-soon requests sort
first. Filter by status, priority, project manager, text or _Overdue only_.

1. **Triage.** Open a request, choose the project manager (and optionally priority, an SLA
   override and a note) and press _Triage and assign_. From the queue you can also select several
   inquiries and use _Triage and assign PM_.
2. **Quote.** _Draft a quote_ from a quotation template or from your own lines (description,
   quantity, unit amount), with a reviewed tax treatment and deposit percentage. _Issue to
   customer_ sends that version and moves the request to _quoted_; _New version_ supersedes it.
   The customer accepts or rejects in their portal; acceptance creates the deposit or service
   invoice.
3. **Transitions** (pause, resume, cancel, reject, start work, deliver…) are offered only when
   valid and ask for a reason where the workflow requires one.
4. **Notes** have an explicit visibility: internal (staff only) or customer-visible.
5. **Documents** lists files attached to the request; only scanned, clean files can be
   downloaded (short-lived link).
6. **Assignments** propose inspectors, surveyors or partners; access starts when they accept and
   ends immediately on revocation.
7. **Appointments and visits** shows linked bookings; _Book appointment_ opens the staff booking
   page pre-linked to the request. _Create project from this request_ appears once the quote is
   accepted.

## Customers and properties

- `/admin/customers`: organisations with members, properties, requests and invoices. Contact
  fields are masked unless your role holds `customers.read_sensitive`. _Open support ticket_
  starts a support conversation with chosen members.
- `/admin/properties`: every private property with units, parcels, linked projects and documents.
  Owner authority documents can be verified or rejected (with a reason).

## Projects (`/admin/projects/{id}`)

Tabs: Overview, Budget, Schedule, Milestones, Visits, Reports, Evidence, Defects, Change orders,
Permits, Design.

- **Budget:** create budget versions (from a quote, build rate × area, a BOQ or a total with
  contingency); staff approve or reject a version; record commitments and actuals. Variance and
  forecast use approved figures only.
- **Schedule:** edit tasks and dependencies as a new version with a reason; the computed schedule
  and any missing inputs are shown.
- **Milestones:** record progress (inspector estimate), submit for customer acceptance, and
  _Authorise payment (finance)_. These are three different decisions.
- **Visits:** schedule site visits for an inspector, review submitted visits (not your own), cancel
  with a reason.
- **Reports:** _Draft report_, then on the report page _Submit for review_ to a named reviewer who
  is not the author. The reviewer approves or requests changes; someone other than the author
  releases it to the customer. The review queue is at `/admin/reports`.
- **Evidence:** approve files for the customer or restrict them.
- **Defects, Permits, Design options** follow the same pattern: create, then move through the
  offered states.
- **Change orders:** _Draft change order_ (cost delta may be negative) → _Submit for approval_ →
  _Approve (staff)_ or _Reject_. The approved budget changes only when every approval the policy
  requires (staff and, if set, the customer) is present.

## Assignments (`/admin/assignments`)

- _Workload_ shows per person: active, proposed, completed, declined/revoked assignments, open
  requests they manage and visits/appointments in the next seven days.
- _All assignments_ lists every assignment with _Activate_, _Complete_ and _Revoke_ (reason
  required).
- _Calendar_ is the week view of appointments and site visits (Africa/Lagos).

## Finance (`/admin/finance`)

- **Invoices:** filter by status, organisation or service request. _New invoice_ creates a manual
  invoice with lines, per-line tax and optional immediate issue; the preview total is recomputed
  by the server. On an invoice: _Issue invoice_, _Void_ (unpaid only, reason required), _Issue
  credit note_, payment attempts with _Verify with provider_, _Request refund_ on a successful
  payment, bank transfer declarations, receipts and credit notes. Select drafts in the list to
  issue them in bulk.
- **Bank transfers:** oldest declarations first. _Confirm_ asks for the amount actually on the bank
  statement (it may differ from the declaration) and then allocates it and issues a receipt;
  _Reject_ requires a note for the customer. An uploaded slip is never cleared money.
- **Reconciliation:** gateway attempts still initialized, pending, uncertain or reversed, with
  _Verify now_, and the exceptions found by the reconciliation job.
- **Refunds:** approve or reject requested refunds. The requester cannot approve their own refund;
  a refund is settled only when the provider confirms it.
- **Credit notes & receipts, Chart of accounts, Journals:** read-only. Journals show every line,
  whether the journal balances, and reversals.
- **Exports:** choose a window and organisation; the page shows whether allocations reconcile to
  receipts before you _Download CSV_ or _Download JSON_.
- **Payouts:** owner distributions from rentals (see below).

## Appointments (`/admin/appointments`)

- Views: Upcoming, Day, Week calendar and Past; filter by organiser, status and kind.
- Row actions: _Confirm_ (pending bookings), _Reschedule_ (pick a new held slot), _Cancel_ (reason,
  guest notified), _Retry calendar sync_ when Google sync or Meet creation failed, and the `.ics`
  file.
- _Book on behalf of a customer_ finds free slots, holds one for a few minutes and books it. The
  booking API records the signed-in staff member as the booking contact, so put the customer's
  name and phone in the notes and link the service request.
- _Google Calendar and Meet_ shows the real provider state: adapter, organiser connections with
  _Check now_, the sync queue and _Run test booking_ (creates and deletes a real event).
  Credentials live in _Integrations → Google Workspace_.
- _Edit availability and time off_ (`/admin/appointments/availability`) replaces a person's weekly
  windows (ISO weekday, start, end, time zone, kinds) and adds or removes leave/blocks. Time off
  that overlaps a booking is refused.

## Messages (`/admin/messages`)

The inbox lists conversations (unread first by activity). With `messages.read_all` you can read
threads you are not in; _Join conversation_ to reply. The reply box has _Internal note_: those
messages are visible to staff participants only. Participants can be added (staff or members of
the customer organisation) or removed; _Close conversation_ stops replies. _New internal
conversation_ starts a staff-only thread; customer conversations start from the customer page.

## Partners (`/admin/partners`)

The directory shows each partner's type, what was verified and until when, coverage,
availability, disclosed conflicts, and performance counts (assignments, tender invitations, bids,
awards). _Verification queue_ opens `/admin/access/partners`.

## Tenders (`/admin/tenders`)

1. _New tender_ creates a draft: organisation, scope, timeline in the tender's display zone and
   weighted criteria (must sum to 100). The timeline is checked as you type ("Submission deadline
   must be after Release.") and again by the server.
2. On the tender: _Edit draft_, _Publish_, _Issue addendum / extend deadline_ (deadlines only move
   later), _Close bidding_ (refused before the deadline) and _Cancel tender_ (reason).
3. _Invitations_: invite partners (unverified partners are flagged). _Q&A_: answer questions and
   publish answers to every invitee (anonymised).
4. _Bids_: while sealed, only who submitted and when is visible. After closing, _Open sealed bids_
   requires `bids.open_sealed`, a verified authenticator and a reason (audited); _Start
   evaluation_ also opens them.
5. _Evaluation_: the matrix shows each bid's amount, duration, average score per criterion,
   weighted score and rank; _Score_ records your 0–100 scores. _Disqualify_ needs a reason.
6. _Award_: _Decide award_ while evaluating, then _Publish award_; partners learn the outcome only
   on publication.

## Procurement (`/admin/procurement`)

- _New RFQ_ creates a draft with items in your units. _Issue RFQ_ sets the deadline and invites
  supplier accounts; _Record a response_ captures a quote from a supplier with or without an
  account, including the supplier's declared unit conversion.
- The delivered-cost comparison normalises prices to the RFQ units. A line priced in another unit
  without a conversion is shown as unknown and the response is not ranked.
- _Create PO_ from a response (staff-measured conversions are asked for any unconverted line),
  _Issue to supplier_, _Record delivery_ per line, _Accept delivery_, and discrepancies (short,
  damaged, wrong spec) moving to supplier notified → resolved, credited or returned with a
  resolution note.
- The supplier directory shows facilities and what evidence exists; a listed facility carries no
  price unless a reviewed quote is on file.

## Rentals and maintenance (`/admin/rentals`)

- **Leases:** _New lease_ (draft) → _Send for signature_ / _Activate_ (generates the rent schedule)
  → _Renew_, _Terminate_ (reason) or _End now_. The lease page shows balance and arrears ageing,
  charges (_Add charge_), the schedule with invoices, parties (_Invite party_, _Revoke access_),
  notices and linked work orders. Rent collected is held for the owner, not SimplexD revenue.
- **Work orders:** a board by stage with SLA flags (breached work is highlighted). On a work order:
  _Triage_, _Assign contractor_, _Start work_, _Request owner approval of cost_, _Attach evidence_,
  _Complete_ (evidence required), _Verify_ (posts the recoverable expense), _Close_, _Reject_ or
  _Cancel_ with a reason. Owners approve costs in their portal.
- **Owner statements:** _Generate statement_ for a period → _Reconcile_ (posts fee and recovery
  journals and succeeds only if every figure matches) → _Issue to owner_ → _Propose payout_.
- **Payouts:** _First approval_ (not the proposer) → _Second approval_ (a different approver) →
  _Mark submitted to bank_ → _Record settlement_ with the bank reference, or _Mark failed_.
- **Rent invoicing run** runs the hourly rent job on demand and shows its result.

## Known limitations

- Staff bookings are recorded with the staff member as the contact (booking API); the customer's
  details go in the notes.
- Staff cannot upload files from the console; customers and inspectors upload from their portals.
- The navigation still labels some built sections with a release wave; that label comes from the
  shell configuration.
